# Research workflow and production history

## Research design and evidence collection

I created the research tree and defined the scope, content and organization of the source files. I selected the companies and transferable features, then used AI to help research the web, gather data, summarize material and populate the source collection. I read and analyzed that evidence and used it to develop the commercial and product insights in the report.

GPT 5.6 Sol contributed mainly to data gathering and summarization. I also used GPT Astra at high usage for research, source-file completion and drafting based on my analysis. These roles overlapped across the project.

## Working with the source material

I initially uploaded the sources to NotebookLM and connected it to Claude Cowork, using NotebookLM as a source database to reduce context overhead. Its summaries did not preserve enough detail for this case. I discarded that report and moved the source files into the report project to work directly with Astra at medium-to-high usage.

## Analysis and creative development

I played Squad Busters, Clean It! and All in Hole, documented their mechanics and provided screenshots from start to finish. My creative additions drew on my game experience and industry understanding, informed by the research collected for this project. AI helped organize the observations into technical recommendations, implementation considerations and validation measures.

Campaign analysis used the supplied raw data. AI-assisted Python recalculation checked segment and aggregate ratios against the supplied metric file. Product and prospect research used source-linked evidence; proposed benefits and test plans were kept distinct from observed behavior and measured results.

## Report production and verification

GPT Astra 6 at medium-to-high usage supported report writing, structure, visuals and live revisions. AI also assisted the brief-to-deliverable mapping, source register, calculation appendix, screenshot selection, vector wireframe, file hashing, source attachments and rendered-layout checks.

I reviewed each stage, supplied interpretation and creative direction, and finalized the work. The final production pass aligned the report and supporting statements in first-person or neutral language and removed internal review commentary.

## Division of labor

| Area | My contribution | AI contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | Research tree, scope, company and feature selection | Research organization and source completion |
| Interpretation | Evidence analysis, commercial judgment and creative insights | Synthesis, alternatives and technical elaboration |
| Gameplay | Playthrough, descriptions, critique and screenshots | Organization, visual inspection and recommendation drafting |
| Quantitative work | Interpretation and investigation priorities | Recalculation, reconciliation and diagnostic tables |

I initiated, approved and finalized the manual intervention logs. I also retained responsibility for the insights, judgments and finalization.
