## Quantitative controls

Puzzle Game X: $20,000 divided by 7,160 installs gives $2.793296 CPI. Grocery App Y: $14,400 divided by 1,810 first orders gives $7.955801 CPA. Display rounding is applied after calculation.

Segment percentages and CPAs are not averaged to obtain campaign totals. Average time is not aggregated because its measurement base is unspecified. Revenue, retention and margin are absent, so the analysis does not claim ROAS, profitability or downstream customer quality.

The campaign tables are descriptive. Market, operating system, device, placement and attribution differences require investigation before causal conclusions or substantial budget changes.

## Evidence scope

Gameplay timing, sound and behavior come from my playthrough; screenshots document visible states. Recommendations are proposed changes and test plans, not measured performance gains.

Company profiles provide self-reported size bands. Ad-intelligence records establish dated observations in named markets, not audited spend or exhaustive coverage. Buying roles are role-based prospecting recommendations.

The Sensor Tower section describes a proposed workflow under the brief's API-access assumption. No Sensor Tower API was called. No recruiter outreach, campaign launch or budget change forms part of this research.
