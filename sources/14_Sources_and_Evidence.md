# Complete source register and verification boundaries

Research date: **2 October 2026**. This register covers the sources actually used in the research kit, including the supplied documents, official product/company material, technical references and required-but-uninspected artifacts. It excludes irrelevant search results. URLs are research references, not proof of every possible claim about the organization.

## How to use this register

Source IDs in the guides resolve here. “Retrieved” means content was available through web retrieval; it does not mean the service was used, the app installed, or an ad campaign verified. “Search-retrieved” means the search service returned substantive source content; inspect the full page before using a consequential claim in the final submission. “Blocked” sources cannot support claims about their unseen contents. Marketing claims remain vendor claims.

Public pages usually had no clear publication date. The date above is the research/access date, not their publication date. Refresh time-sensitive claims at execution. All recommendations, concepts, prioritization and proposed architecture in this kit are original analysis unless attributed otherwise. All campaign numbers are calculations from S02, not external benchmarks.
