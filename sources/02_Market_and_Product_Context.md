# Playable Factory: researched context and commercial logic

## Verified product baseline

PF markets custom playable creative work and self-service tools. Its current public pages describe Ready as template-based creation, Flex as video-to-interactive-ad creation, and Data as creative analytics. The manual's product list includes Insight; the `/insight` URL returned 404 during this research. Do not assume that Data is formally a renamed Insight without confirmation. Choose Ready or Flex to avoid making product identity the weak point of Task 2. [S03–S07]

| Product | Publicly described capability | Implication for this case |
|---|---|---|
| Ready | Customizable playable templates, asset/branding/difficulty editing, export, duplication/A/B testing | Do not propose basic template editing or basic A/B testing as a new feature |
| Flex | Video-based interactive creatives with buttons, overlays, tutorials and assets | Useful if a client has strong video assets and needs quick interactive variants |
| Data | Metrics, variant comparisons, historical analysis and heatmaps | Do not pitch simple heatmaps or comparison charts as an undiscovered gap |
| Insight | Named in brief; current precise product identity not verified | Avoid unsupported product equivalence |

Vendor statements about improved performance are marketing claims, not independent benchmarks. Do not promise a universal CPI or ROAS improvement based on them.

## How the value proposition changes by client

For a game, the playable can demonstrate the actual interaction and let a potential player self-select before installation. The business question is whether the creative brings suitable players at an acceptable acquisition cost. An install is an intermediate outcome if retained-player value and monetization ultimately pay for acquisition.

For an app or brand, the playable should rehearse a customer benefit: building a useful basket, choosing a meal, configuring an item or understanding a service. The interaction should lead naturally to a real conversion. A grocery app can acquire low-value coupon users as easily as valuable customers; first-order CPA needs repeat-order and margin context before scale decisions.

These are strategic interpretations. PF's sector pages establish that non-gaming use cases are within its positioning; they do not prove any proposed client's incremental gain. [S08]

## Measurement language

- **CPI:** ad spend divided by attributed installs.
- **First-order CPA:** ad spend divided by attributed first orders. In this case, it excludes unknown creative, agency, incentive and operating costs; do not call it fully loaded CAC.
- **CTR:** specify the denominator. In this kit it is CTA clicks divided by impressions.
- **Engagement:** use named actions and denominators instead of a vague score.
- **ROAS:** attributed revenue divided by ad spend; impossible from the supplied sheet.
- **Incrementality:** additional outcomes caused by advertising relative to a counterfactual; not established by attribution counts alone.

## Production implications

Treat hook, first successful interaction, progression, payoff and CTA as different stages. A strong mini-game that does not explain the product or creates accidental clicks can still be a poor ad. For non-gaming concepts, avoid game jargon and demonstrate the service benefit clearly.

Production exports depend on the actual network and placement. IAB MRAID is a rich-media interface standard, not a universal guarantee of export compatibility. Google and AppLovin maintain distinct asset guidance. Verify the relevant current requirements if building a prototype; do not promise one identical artifact works everywhere. [S09–S11]

## What to research before making a claim

| Claim | Acceptable evidence | Inadequate substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Company owns/publishes app | Store publisher plus official corporate/app source | Similar name |
| Studio fits size rule | Current corporate/ownership evidence and reasonable scale explanation | Old independent-studio label |
| Recent US/Europe UA | Dated ad record with target-country coverage | Downloads, app update, funding or UA job posting |
| Product lacks feature | Demo/documentation and explicit absence confirmation where possible | Feature not mentioned on homepage |
| Playable has friction | Direct observation and preferably timestamp/screenshot | Main game's reputation |
| Change improves conversion | Controlled measurement or a clearly labeled hypothesis | Higher engagement alone |
