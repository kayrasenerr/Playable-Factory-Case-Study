it's a two phased game. the first mission is cleaning the restaurant floor with a vacumm that cleans dirty surface and picks up small trash on the floor. dragging the avatar on a spot automatically cleans the spot the whole floor is covered with dirt and trash, but cleaning around 20% of it finishes the first mission. second mission is picking up fast food trash from tables, that replenishes all the time. there are angry customers waiting on the cashier that are not related to the avatar or game objectives. there is also a dumping mechanic as the avatar can dump the trash he collected to big trash containers on two sides of the restaurant, but doing so is completely optional. there is a picked-up trash counter on the left (1/4, 2/4 ...). each picked up trash is removed from this count after 10 seconds, so the winning condition is picking 4 objects in the last 10 seconds. after that, win screen appears.
