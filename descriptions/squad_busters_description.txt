the mini game starts by showing the final boss, panning through the map (that we will go through) and stop on our character. there are 3 coins and 2 skeletons waiting in front of a chest. the avatar has a blue attack range visual. player drags to move and stops to shoot enemies in range. killing enemies rewards emeralds and coins (both converted to coins, an emerald = a coin with a different visual). chests require coins to open, and show a selection of characters. only 1 is choosable (same character as the avatar). choosing it spawns a duplicate avatar that moves and shoots along the original avatar, they share the same blue attack range. next obstacle is 3 skeletons, which gives more coins for another chest, which also only enables picking the same character. since there are now 3 of the character, they fuse into 1, bigger avatar of the same character. moving forward reveals the final boss, which kgives more coins upon killing. after collecting all coins, the mini game ends.
avatar cant fall from cliffs, and can pass from visible obstacles (skeletons, chest, trees etc.). enemies hit the avatar but avatar has no health and doesnt take any damage. unopened chests are shown as a mini icon and coin requirement on screen, revealing their direction (if they can be seen on the screen, the icon is placed on top of chest)
